THE FIT SYSTEM

Health tools should work together.

FIT connects what you buy, what you eat, how you train, and how you feel—so each next step has context.

01FIT Scan

Food scores with context.

Scan a barcode or label and see nutrition, processing, ingredient evidence, allergy conflicts, and how the product fits each of your goals.

02FIT Fuel

Meals that become a real grocery list.

Approve recipes before they reach your calendar. FIT combines exact quantities, pantry inventory, package sizes, and saved store locations.

03FIT Train

A plan built around your actual week.

TOBURT creates dated workouts from your goals, experience, schedule, gym, equipment, and limitations—then adjusts from what you log.

04FIT Progress

One view of the routine.

Track meals, nutrients, workouts, sets, check-ins, and trends without turning a single score or scale reading into the whole story.
