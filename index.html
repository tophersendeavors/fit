COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers,
including the limits.

What is FIT by TOBURT?

FIT is a connected food, nutrition, meal-planning, grocery, workout, and accountability app. TOBURT is the AI coach layer that plans workouts, explains ingredients, and responds to how each set felt.

Can I download FIT right now?

FIT is in a private iPhone beta with invited testers. Use the beta request form above to be considered for the current test group.

How does FIT score food?

Every scan returns a serving-based food-quality score alongside separate nutrition, processing, and personal-fit scores, with confidence and evidence labels. Your goals cannot inflate a product's underlying score, and missing data stays missing rather than invented.

How does FIT handle allergies?

Saved allergens are checked on every scan and conflicts stay prominent. A conflict can change your personal verdict but never rewrites the objective nutrition calculation. Databases can be incomplete — always check the current physical package.

Does FIT replace a doctor or dietitian?

No. FIT is a general-wellness and educational product. FIT and TOBURT are not doctors, registered dietitians, physical therapists, or emergency services. Stop exercising and seek qualified help for pain or concerning symptoms.

What will FIT cost?

After a 3-day trial, monthly plans are FIT Scanner $6.99, Pro $19.99, Platinum $34.99, and Diamond $49.99. Weekly and discounted annual options are also available. Beta testers are not charged.

Is my health data private?

Health, allergy, body-composition, food, and workout data are private by default and never published automatically. AI requests run through an authenticated server gateway, and permanent account deletion is available in-app.

Which devices does FIT support?

The beta runs on iPhone through TestFlight. Android and iPad timing will be decided after the public iOS release.
