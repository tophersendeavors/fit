01 · SCAN WITH CONTEXT

Know exactly
what you're buying.

Scan a UPC or EAN barcode, enter it by hand, type a produce PLU, or enter the label when no barcode record exists. FIT keeps what the food is separate from how it fits you. Try a product.

01

Four ways in

Camera barcode scanning, manual barcode numbers, common produce PLUs, or a photographed or typed Nutrition Facts and ingredient label.

02

Four perspectives, not one verdict

Nutrition, gut support, processing, and personal fit — with food-quality scoring shown separately from goal compatibility.

03

Warnings that matter to you

Known allergies, eating-style conflicts, and explicitly excluded ingredients are checked on every scan. Open any ingredient for a plain-language explanation.

04

Official recall records

Review food-safety and recall information drawn from official FDA and USDA FSIS records, then add an approved product to your basket or scan the next one.

WHAT FIT WILL NOT CLAIM FIT does not infer pesticide, contaminant, glyphosate, aflatoxin, cancer, or chemical exposure from a barcode alone. When evidence is unavailable, FIT says so.

03 · THE GROCERY LIST

One quantity per ingredient,
not one line per recipe.

If three meals call for ground turkey, FIT combines those uses into a single store-ready amount, subtracts what your pantry already holds, and estimates packages and leftovers.

Shop without losing your place

Check items off as you go, and scan a product against a selected list item without the scanner getting stuck on it.

Your store, remembered

Optional price, store, aisle, shelf, and location notes, plus preferred products and package sizes — kept for next week.

A basket for comparing

Keep scanned products side by side in a shopping basket before you decide which one goes in the cart.

CONVERSIONS ARE NOT GUESSED

When a recipe measures in cups and the package is in grams, FIT asks you to confirm rather than inventing a conversion.

05 · PROGRESS, APPLE HEALTH & DEVICES

Your numbers,
honestly labelled.

Connect Apple Health with your permission and FIT reads supported activity information — steps, active energy, exercise minutes, sleep, weight, and compatible body-composition measurements — without asking you to reconnect every week.

01

Weekly check-ins, your units

Pounds or kilograms, feet and inches or centimeters. Progress charts follow your own check-in schedule.

02

Advanced scale readings, even manually

Record weight, BMI, body fat, skeletal muscle, fat-free mass, subcutaneous and visceral fat, body water, muscle and bone mass, protein percentage, BMR, metabolic age, and scale body type — with brand, model, and measurement source stored alongside.

ABOUT DEVICE DATA Device availability differs by manufacturer. FIT can read only the data Apple Health or an authorized integration makes available. Smart-scale body-composition readings are estimates and are not medical measurements.

Scores are estimates

COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers,
including the limits.

Is FIT only a food scanner?

No. Scanning is the entry point, but FIT also connects food logging, verified meal planning, grocery organization, workout planning, workout logging, progress tracking, supported Apple Health data, and Community accountability.

Does AI freely invent meal nutrition?

No. Production meal plans use nutrition-verified, published recipes. AI may interpret a request, but approved ingredients, substitutions, allergy rules, realistic portions, and recalculated nutrition remain enforced by the application.

Can I use FIT without following an AI meal plan?

Yes. Members can log their own food, scan products, enter meals manually, and build their own routines.

Can FIT connect to my scale?

FIT reads compatible measurements made available through Apple Health and supports manual entry for additional smart-scale readings. The exact information available depends on the scale manufacturer and its Apple Health integration.

Does FIT track running pace?

Yes. Enter distance and elapsed time and FIT automatically calculates and saves pace. Continuous GPS route tracking is planned for a future update.

Is Community public by default?

Members choose their privacy setting. Private health and nutrition information is not posted automatically. Public profiles display only information the member intentionally shares.

Is TOBURT medical advice?

No. TOBURT provides educational guidance and app support. It is not a medical provider or emergency service.

What will FIT cost?

Planned memberships are FIT Scanner, FIT Pro, FIT Platinum, and FIT Diamond, offered weekly, monthly, or annually. Prices and inclusions remain subject to final App Store configuration, and beta testers currently receive Diamond-equivalent access without charge.

How do I get into the beta?

Send the beta request form with your name and email. Approved testers receive a TestFlight invite by email along with install instructions.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The current release stage is a private iOS beta through TestFlight, and an Android public release is on the roadmap.
